DAY 21: Four Pillars of OOP
|
+-- OVERVIEW
|   +-- The four commonly taught OOP pillars are encapsulation, abstraction, inheritance and polymorphism. They help structure maintainable and reusable applications.
|
+-- CORE CONCEPTS
|   +-- Encapsulation groups data and methods and controls access by convention or properties.
|   +-- Abstraction exposes essential behavior while hiding implementation details.
|   +-- Inheritance lets a class reuse or extend another class.
|   +-- Polymorphism lets different objects respond to the same interface.
|
+-- PRACTICE
|   +-- Give a real-world example of each OOP pillar.
|   +-- Create a base Employee class and a Manager subclass.
|   +-- Demonstrate polymorphism with two payment classes.
