DAY 08: Tuples
|
+-- OVERVIEW
|   +-- Tuples are ordered, immutable collections. They are useful for fixed groups of related values and can be unpacked into variables.
|
+-- CORE CONCEPTS
|   +-- Create tuples with commas; parentheses are optional.
|   +-- A one-item tuple needs a trailing comma.
|   +-- Tuples support indexing, slicing and iteration.
|   +-- Tuple unpacking assigns elements to variables.
|
