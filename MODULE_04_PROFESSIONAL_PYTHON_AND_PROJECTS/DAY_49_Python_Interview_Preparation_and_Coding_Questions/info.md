**Module:** Real-World Projects

## 1. Overview
Review core concepts and practice explaining decisions clearly. Focus on readable solutions, complexity, edge cases and examples from your projects.

## 2. Learning objectives
By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:
- Explain concepts in your own words.
- Discuss trade-offs rather than memorizing definitions.
- State assumptions and test edge cases.
- Describe your project, your contribution and what you learned.
